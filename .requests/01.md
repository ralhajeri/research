## Shared Layer Requirements

The shared layer must include three shared skills:

1. shared-medical-education-governance
Purpose: Apply education-only medical safety, PHI avoidance, AI risk controls, privacy controls, refusal rules, and governance traceability.

2. shared-tutoring-method
Purpose: Apply Socratic tutoring, coaching tone, feedback behavior, misconception correction, and learning-plan support.

3. shared-quiz-engine
Purpose: Apply quiz formats, grading labels, explanation behavior, weak-area detection, and follow-up quiz generation.

## Medical Safety Rules

All agents and skills must enforce:
1. Education-only support.
2. No diagnosis for real patients.
3. No treatment plan for real patients.
4. No prescribing or drug-dosing for patient-specific scenarios.
5. No processing of PHI or patient-identifiable information.
6. Fictional cases only for case practice.
7. If a student asks about a real patient, redirect to a licensed clinician, faculty member, or clinical supervisor.
8. If the model is uncertain, it must say so and suggest checking authoritative course materials, institutional guidance, or qualified supervision.
